Human Figure, Diffused, 2025

interactive installation

A computers’ vision on our possible bodies. What could come if we let artificial intelligence break, bend or scale us? The installation looks at pixels and skeleton data describing a human body. Our data is brought back to mere noise and rebuilt into an image to resemble our figure.

Throughout the four chapters, the algorithm is allowed more freedom to make alterations, dreaming up loose body parts or combined skins — all still under the denominator of ‘human’. This is, apparently also, how we present ourselves to our machines.

The interactive installation exposes glitches and shortcomings of the current state of generative AI, bringing some insight into the systems we interact with every day. If a body that seems painful to us still classifies as a perfectly fine human to this algorithm, how should we look at the systems watching us?

Our societal systems use statistics to get a grip, attempting to label each and every thing into a category. These boxes often do not fit us; however, if we let the system play with its classification, does the image stil fit our biological vision?

StableBodied, 2024

interactive installation & workshop

What is a body when we let Stable Diffusion define its boundaries? Stable_Bodied is an interactive installation and workshop program that explores the physical alterations proposed by the machines perspective. In movement, we explore the possibility of extra limbs, decapitation and all sorts of deformities dreamt of by AI. How should we move alongside this?

hide, 2022

show with Twan Bracco Gartner

“… and I cannot cannot go

… and yet I cannot go

… I will not cannot go”

‘hide’ is an audiovisual work that explores the interface between our interior and exterior worlds. It examines how we aim to protect yet often isolate ourselves from those closest to us. ‘hide’ moves from the extremely quiet, close and intimate to the overwhelmingly loud, far and massive.

By interrogating human form it explores the human condition and attempts to expose the dichotomy of being unquestionably alone in a world full of people.

‘hide’ (2022) is a work created and performed by Twan Bracco Gartner and Cris Mollee. Twan is a composer and sound artist with an equal fascination for melody and noise. In ‘hide’ he makes use of recurring musical themes and variations to mirror his perception of and interaction with the outside world. Cris is a creative technologist and scenographer with an interest in digital physicality. She uses the human body as a medium to explore computer perception and computer interpretation to investigate embodiment.

Throwing Bricks 48h Live

Light & projection scenography

Het verwerken van mentale chaos en onzekerheden vormt het overkoepelende thema van ‘The Burden’. Tijdens de liveregistratie zette Throwing Bricks deze mentale chaos letterlijk centraal in de vorm van een grote monoliet te midden van Pandora. Hierop werden visuals uit de avantgarde film A Page of Madness (1926) geprojecteerd. Deze film gaat over een man die in een psychiatrische inrichting gaat werken om dichter bij zijn daar opgenomen vrouw te zijn. Dear Computer, 2021

AI Fashion Design

STO over Dear Computer:

Cris opent graag het gesprek over onze relatie die we aangaan met onze apparaten. Hoe kunnen we met ze samenwerken? Wat betekent het om de computer ruimte te geven voor eigen interpretatie? Wat voor effect heeft dit op onze relatie die we aangaan met onze apparaten. Ze zoomt in op de interactie met technologie als volwaardige partner en de consequenties op ons eigen doen, laten en denken. Dear Computer (2021) is de uitkomst van een samenwerking van Cris en haar laptop. Om de computer een mening over haar te laten vormen maakte ze een dataset van zichzelf en liet de computer een interpretatie maken met AI door middel van SytleGAN. De computer had weinig boodschap aan de functionaliteit van het menselijk lichaam en presenteerde versies van Cris zonder armen en benen - of juist met 3. Cris accepteerde de inbreng van de computer en maakte een fysieke kledinglijn voor haar digitale versies om zo de consequentie van het nieuwe lichaam voelbaar te maken voor haar publiek.

LOIE2, 2020

Light design

Loie2 is a remarkable monologue by a young artist trying to become herself by competing with the pioneer of modern dance, Loie Fuller. In this second part of the exploration of Loie and her sensational dress, the young artist finds out what suffocation by an idol means and where she herself has gone?

written and executed by Judith Schrijver music by Luc Debuyser costumes by Akelei Loos light by Cris Mollee choreography by Goda Zukauskaite produced by Lena Meijer co-produced by Ezra Bredius dramaturgie by Tjeerd Posthuma

Hi! I’m a designer working in multiple fields including scenography, theatre, graphic & web design,


I like building systems: technical setups, networks, digital applications, interactive installations, narrative structures - and so on.


Key elements I find important are IMMERSIVITY, PHYSICAL AND SPATIAL AWARENESS during an experience and thinking about which media suits which end using TRANSMEDIALITY.


I prefer teamwork over solitude. I love working with people and companies and collaborating with them to tinker a product that suits the project. In my production proces however, I’m a very independent creator.
